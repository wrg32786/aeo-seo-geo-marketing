# Ethical Source Earning

Use this module when the target answer relies on third-party sources or when owned-page changes alone cannot solve retrieval, trust, recommendation, or corroboration gaps.

## 1. Principle: map before outreach

Do not start with “get more mentions.” Start with the target prompt portfolio.

For each prompt and platform, record:

- source domains and URLs;
- source type: owned, editorial, review, directory, forum, Reddit, video, academic, registry, maps, feed, product database;
- whether the source is cited, merely retrieved, or appears to influence wording;
- whether the source mentions, recommends, criticizes, or omits the brand;
- freshness and geographic relevance;
- whether a realistic path to inclusion exists.

Aggregate this into a source-chain matrix:

| Intent | Platform/mode | Repeated source types | Repeated domains | Competitor presence | Brand presence | Earnable gap | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Only invest where the source type recurs, reaches actual customers, or supplies a missing fact.

## 2. Source-earning priority

Prioritize opportunities by:

`fit × audience value × source recurrence × evidence quality × attainability ÷ maintenance and reputation risk`

Use qualitative `high`, `medium`, or `low`; do not invent decimal precision.

### High-value assets

- original datasets and reproducible research;
- benchmarks with methods and raw data;
- useful calculators, tools, templates, or open-source software;
- expert commentary tied to demonstrated experience;
- standards, registries, specifications, and public records;
- customer case evidence with named scope and permission;
- definitive documentation, compatibility tables, or troubleshooting data;
- transparent comparison frameworks;
- high-quality visual demonstrations or videos with transcripts;
- community answers that solve a real problem without requiring a click.

The asset must be worth referencing even if no AI system existed.

## 3. Channel adapters

### Earned editorial and expert commentary

Use when target answers repeatedly cite industry publications, news, trade media, or expert sources.

Process:

1. Match the publication’s audience and recurring question.
2. Offer a verifiable fact, dataset, demonstration, or qualified expert—not a generic company announcement.
3. Provide concise claims with primary evidence and limitations.
4. Disclose commercial interest.
5. Preserve an owned source page that reporters and readers can verify.
6. Track publication, wording, link, citation, mention, sentiment, referrals, and qualified outcomes separately.

Do not mass-send templated pitches or manufacture news.

### Reviews and comparison platforms

Use when target commercial prompts repeatedly rely on review sites or comparison databases.

- Claim and complete official profiles.
- Keep category, pricing, integrations, service area, and screenshots current.
- Ask real customers for honest reviews without dictating language or conditioning incentives on positivity.
- Reply factually to criticism and correct data errors through platform processes.
- Do not create employee, agency, or fake-customer reviews.
- Distinguish paid placement from organic review presence.

Review volume is not the goal; credible decision evidence is.

### Directories, registries, and marketplaces

Use when the source is authoritative for the entity type: licensing boards, professional associations, app marketplaces, package registries, map products, government directories, commerce marketplaces, or standards bodies.

- Prefer authoritative vertical registries over generic submission farms.
- Keep name, identifiers, location, category, credentials, URLs, and status consistent.
- Remove duplicates and stale listings.
- Document verification and renewal dates.
- Do not buy irrelevant directory links for a synthetic footprint.

### YouTube and video platforms

Use when demonstrations, product use, procedures, events, or visual comparisons recur in source chains.

- Create a video that directly answers the target job.
- State the tested product/version/date and disclose affiliation.
- Use an accurate title and description.
- Provide captions, transcript, chapters, source links, and a corresponding owned page.
- Show primary evidence on screen when possible.
- Track video retrieval, citation, brand mention, view quality, referral, and assisted conversion separately.

Do not publish a video merely to duplicate an article with stock footage.

### Partnerships and integrations

Use when customers discover products through ecosystem pages, integration marketplaces, implementation partners, or co-authored case studies.

- Maintain reciprocal factual pages with consistent product names and capabilities.
- Verify integration status and version.
- Publish a working example or joint case outcome.
- Avoid reciprocal link pages with no user purpose.

### Academic and research sources

Use when the subject genuinely requires scholarly evidence.

- Contribute data, methods, code, replication, or domain expertise.
- Publish preprints and repositories only when research standards are met.
- Do not create pseudo-academic papers for citation laundering.
- Distinguish peer review, accepted proceedings, preprint, position paper, and vendor study.

## 4. Reddit and forums

Reddit can be retrieved heavily, cited lightly, favored by some Google surfaces, or nearly absent from other engines. The pattern changes by query, period, and platform. Treat it as a conditional community channel, not an AI backlink service.

### Activation criteria

Use Reddit only when at least one is true:

- Reddit appears repeatedly in the target prompt’s cited or retrieved source chain;
- the audience genuinely asks the target question in a relevant subreddit;
- the business can provide first-hand, non-promotional expertise;
- the thread itself can create customer research, product feedback, or trust even without a link.

Do not post solely because an industry report says Reddit is “favored by AI.”

### Recon

Before participating:

1. Read subreddit rules, moderator guidance, pinned posts, and recent removals.
2. Search the subreddit for the question, brand, competitors, and destination domain.
3. Inspect whether links are normal, restricted, filtered, or banned.
4. Determine whether the account has a legitimate participation history.
5. Note recurring language, objections, use cases, and evidence standards.
6. Decide whether a comment on an existing thread is more useful than a new post.

### Contribution standard

A compliant contribution:

- answers the question in the comment or post itself;
- includes relevant experience, constraints, examples, or data;
- names commercial affiliation clearly when material;
- mentions the brand only when necessary to the answer;
- links only when the linked page supplies evidence, a tool, a full method, or necessary detail;
- uses the exact canonical URL and no tracking clutter unless permitted;
- welcomes correction and avoids defensive sales language;
- remains maintainable if the linked facts change.

### Prohibited behavior

- fake customers, fake experts, fake local residents, or multiple personas;
- bought, rented, or aged accounts;
- coordinated upvotes/downvotes or employee voting rings;
- mass posting, near-duplicate comments, AI comment farms, or cross-subreddit blasting;
- undisclosed agency/client/affiliate relationships;
- link drops with thin commentary;
- reviving old threads only to insert a brand;
- fabricated anecdotes or “I discovered this great tool” scripts;
- private-message spam;
- attempts to evade automoderator or moderator decisions.

Reddit’s platform-wide spam rule prohibits repeated or unsolicited actions that negatively affect users or communities. Individual subreddits may ban all promotion or impose stricter norms.

### Measurement

Track:

- post/comment survival and moderator action;
- useful replies, saves, and substantive engagement—not vote totals alone;
- thread indexing and canonical URL;
- brand mention and sentiment;
- whether the thread is retrieved, cited, or absorbed by each named engine;
- direct referral and qualified conversion;
- support, product, or research insights created;
- reputation costs or negative framing.

Never report a Reddit link as a success unless it created the intended human or measured discovery outcome.

## 5. Self-promotional owned and contributed content

Small live-engine experiments suggest self-promotional listicles can sometimes create a new brand-category association, but citations may be temporary and the cited page may recommend a competitor instead. Use only as a disclosed, controlled experiment.

Required controls:

- brand has a documented category-association gap;
- page has a real user purpose;
- ranking/comparison criteria are defined before conclusions;
- competitors are represented accurately;
- ownership and commercial interest are disclosed;
- recommendation share and competitor backfire are measured;
- maintenance owner keeps every listed fact current;
- site reputation abuse and host-publisher policies are checked.

Prefer independent corroboration over a field of self-authored “best” pages.

## 6. Brand/entity consistency without manufacturing consensus

Build a canonical entity record:

```yaml
entity_name: ""
alternate_names: []
entity_type: ""
canonical_url: ""
logo_url: ""
founding_or_launch_date: ""
location_or_service_area: ""
category: ""
offer_summary: ""
verified_identifiers: []
official_profiles: []
current_products_or_services: []
claims_allowed: []
claims_prohibited: []
owner: ""
verified_at: "YYYY-MM-DD"
```

Use this record to keep owned pages, schema, feeds, profiles, directories, press materials, and partner pages accurate. Consistency is not repetition for its own sake; it prevents entity confusion and stale facts.

## 7. Outreach output

Every source-earning recommendation must include:

```yaml
source_type: ""
target_url_or_domain: ""
why_this_source_matters: ""
target_prompt_family: ""
current_competitor_presence: ""
legitimate_inclusion_path: ""
asset_needed: ""
disclosure_required: true
policy_checked: true
evidence: "B|F|O"
risk: "low|medium|high"
owner: ""
success_metric: ""
stop_rule: ""
```

Do not provide a spammy “100 sites to post on” list without current source-chain evidence.
